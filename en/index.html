How it works

No IT project and no extra work for your team. You just review the answers.

1

We build it from your website

Catchly learns your treatments, prices and hours straight from your site. Live within 48 hours after you approve it.

2

We set up forwarding

We arrange forwarding of unanswered calls with your phone provider. You don't have to do anything and your number doesn't change.

3

It answers and captures

It answers in English, takes the name and phone number of anyone who wants to book, and emails them to you instantly.

4

We tune and report

We listen to calls, fine-tune the answers, and send you a monthly summary: how many calls Catchly answered and how many clients it caught.

  • No medical advice — health questions go to your team
  • Month-to-month — no long contracts
  • Callers know who they're talking to — it says it's an automated assistant
  • There's always a human fallback — if Catchly doesn't understand or the caller asks for a person, it transfers the call to a number you choose
